Scope: the public pitch page for HackPrinceton judges (Persuade). Static, always online on *.vercel.app. Never live data.
Audience/job: judges, often on a phone, during or after judging. They should understand what Syl is in seconds, try every feature through example threads, believe it is real and safe, and ask the builder for a seat.
Constraints: the dashboard's world (campus watercolor, Fraunces + Figtree, ink navy, one highlighter yellow, five crew characters); calm, not cluttered, no hype; not Elfie's look; examples labeled; demo video slot hidden until a URL exists; interview section hidden until quotes exist.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The campus is the page: every place on it is a crew member, and tapping a place replays that crew member's real thread. Refuses the phone-mockup-plus-feature-grid landing page.
OWN-WORLD: Full-bleed campus watercolor (arches, hall, bell tower; library and seminar-desk paintings for later stops), cream paper ground, ink navy type and buttons, highlighter-yellow marks, round numbered map pins in the five crew colors, the tilted ink-framed phone.
STORY: Visitors land on the campus, tap pins, watch Syl work; then walk the stops (arches, hall, library, registrar, bell tower) to see every feature and rule; then behind the gates (how it fits, Photon, tracks, stack, next) and ask for a seat.
