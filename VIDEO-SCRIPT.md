# Video script: Syllabus Crew (about 2 minutes)

A general walk-through for the pitch page (`content.js` → `SITE.videoUrl`) and the submission.
Voiceover only. **Everything is filmed on the iPhone**, because the product lives in iMessage and calls your phone: the texts, the card pages (they open in Safari) and the pitch page for the architecture. The MacBook never appears; it's only used off camera to send the first question.
Tracks it serves: **Education** (personal, interactive, accessible), **Photon** (it lives in iMessage, texts first, calls), **Amazon** (Claude on Bedrock, rules in code).

**The frame:** a 16:9 canvas in the page's cream (#f3eee2). The iPhone recording sits in the middle in a thin dark phone frame (#1e2740). Captions to the left of the phone, one short line at a time, ink (#1e2740). On the right, a small progress list: Setup · Syllabus · Quiz · Call · How it's built. Key texts (the proposal, "Done", the recap) get a short 1.5× zoom.
**Voice:** calm, plain, a little dry. No hype words. Read the VO lines as written; change any wording that doesn't sound like you.
**As recorded:** vibe **hype**, name left as the default "Syl", the real NYU syllabus (ACA-UF 9101), the booked block "Monday, Nov 16, 4:00–4:30, The Book of Songs review".

**Order on screen ≠ order filmed.** The video opens with a teaser (the first seconds of block B), then a card "Here's how it got there", then block A from the start.

## What we learned filming (iOS limits)
- **Locking the phone ends a screen recording**, so the teaser is a banner on the Home screen, not the lock screen. Messages banners on, style Persistent; Syl allowed through Do Not Disturb.
- **An incoming call ends a screen recording** too, and screen recordings never capture call audio. So the call scene is C1 (up to the ring), the real audio from ElevenLabs (every drill call is recorded there, clean), and C2 (after hanging up). AirPods on the iPhone for the call: no echo.
- **Recording call audio on the Mac echoes** if the Mac also takes the call (Calls on Other Devices) or the room is bare. The ElevenLabs recording avoids both.
- **The calendar shot** uses the Booked link's "Open in Google Calendar" button, not the iPhone Calendar app (which may lag minutes behind Google).
- **A Reset clears the syllabus and questions.** Before re-recording the quiz: text hi, skip the name, hype, the PDF, then `/demo` → Write quiz questions, and keep every topic at 50%.

## Voiceover
Record after filming, in a quiet room, phone or AirPods about 20 cm away, a bit slower than normal. One clip per line (named 1, 1b, 2, 3…) is easiest; one long take with 2-second pauses also works. If you stumble, say the line again.

## Editing notes
- Waits (the PDF read, the call connecting, the recap) are cut or sped up 4× with a small "about 10 s" label. Never cut inside a reply to make it look faster.
- Music optional, very low, silent under the call audio.
- Export 1080p, upload to YouTube as **Unlisted**, paste the link into `apps/site/content.js` → `SITE.videoUrl`, deploy the page (`cd apps/site && npx -y vercel@65 deploy --prod --yes`).

## Honesty notes (show nothing the product doesn't do)
- Shot 1: the question was sent from the laptop off camera. In real use the Nudger decides when (9 AM to 8 PM, at most 3 a day, never mid-conversation). Optional caption: "sent on cue for the recording".
- Shot 12: the call card is a graphic, labeled "real call audio"; the audio is the actual call.
- It proposes; it never books without YES. Questions have three choices (A, B, C).
- Syl knows the graded items, dates, weights and the readings tied to them, not which reading belongs to each class day.
- It runs on the laptop; the services (Photon, Bedrock, Google, Vercel, ElevenLabs) are real.
